Where to get help

ScrollTax is a tool, not a treatment. It can't diagnose anything and it isn't a replacement for a doctor, a counsellor or a therapist.

If social media is hurting your sleep, your work, your studies or your relationships, it's worth talking to someone. A family doctor is a good first stop. They can point you to services in your area.

If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency number now.

Find a helpline near you

findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines by country. It isn't run by ScrollTax.

Leaving ScrollTax

You can leave at any time, for free. In the app, go to Settings, then Leave ScrollTax. The block comes off after the wait you see there. To remove it straight away, choose Delete my account.
