Common questions

How do I leave? In the app, go to Settings, then Leave ScrollTax. The block comes off after 24 hours. To remove it straight away, choose Delete my account.

I paid but nothing unlocked. Open the app with an internet connection. It sends unfinished payments again. If it still doesn't work, write to us with the time of the payment.

How do I get a refund? Payments go through Apple, so ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The block stopped working. Check that Screen Time access is still allowed for ScrollTax in your iPhone settings.
