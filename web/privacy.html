Privacy policy

ScrollTax blocks social media on your phone and lets you pay to use it for a short time. We try to keep as little about you as we can. This page says what we keep and why.

Who is responsible

[Name and address of the business running ScrollTax. To be filled in before launch.]

What we keep

  • An ID that Apple gives us for your Sign in with Apple account. It isn't your name or email. We don't ask Apple for those.
  • When you created your account, when you confirmed you were old enough, and which version of the terms you accepted.
  • Each payment: Apple's transaction number, the amount, the currency, the time, and whether it was refunded.
  • When each unlock started and ended.
  • If you ask to leave: the time you asked.

What we don't keep

  • Which apps or sites you block. Apple hides this from us, even on your phone.
  • How long you use any app.
  • Your name, email, contacts, location or card details. Apple handles the payment.
  • Why you joined.

Why we keep it

To run your account and your unlocks, to count your daily limit, to deal with refunds, and to follow tax and accounting rules for payments.

How long

Account details stay until you delete your account. When you do, we delete them straight away. Payment records stay for the period tax law requires [number of years to be confirmed], but they are no longer linked to you.

Who else sees it

Apple, which runs the sign in and the payments. Our hosting provider [name and country to be filled in], which stores the data for us. We don't sell data and we don't use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or delete your account in the app (Settings, then Delete my account). You can also complain to your local data protection authority. To ask us anything, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Not a medical product

ScrollTax isn't a treatment for addiction and doesn't give medical advice.
