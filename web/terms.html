Terms of use

The deal

When you register, ScrollTax blocks the apps and sites you chose on your iPhone. To use them, you can pay for an unlock. The price is shown in the app before you pay. Right now an unlock costs €0.99 and lasts 15 minutes, and you can unlock up to 4 times a day. When the time is up, the block comes back by itself.

You choose this

You have to be 18 or over. ScrollTax is only for blocking your own phone. Don't use it to block, or charge, someone else.

Leaving is free

You can leave any time. Ask in Settings and the block comes off after 24 hours. You can change your mind before then. Or you can delete your account in the app, and the block comes off straight away. We don't charge a fee to leave.

Payments and refunds

Payments go through Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple under its rules, at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds a payment, any time you already used stays used.

What we can't promise

  • The block relies on Apple's Screen Time. You can switch it off in your phone's settings, or delete the app. If you do, the block stops working. We can't stop that and we don't charge for it.
  • Apple decides what counts as "social", and what its tools can block. Check that what you want blocked is on your list.
  • The service can have outages, and the app is provided as it is.

Not a medical product

ScrollTax isn't a treatment for addiction and doesn't give medical advice. If social media is causing you real harm, talk to a doctor or counsellor.

Changes

If we change these terms, the app will ask you to accept the new version.
